Refund Policy

We want every customer to have a smooth experience. This Refund Policy explains when refunds, replacements, or cancellations are available for products purchased from Beeway.

01

Overview

At Beeway, customer satisfaction is our priority. Since we primarily sell digital products and services, refunds are available only under specific circumstances described below.

02

Refund Eligibility

Customers may be eligible for a refund if:

  • The purchased product cannot be delivered.
  • The wrong product was delivered.
  • The delivered product is defective or invalid.
  • Delivery fails due to our error.
  • The order cannot be completed because the product is permanently unavailable.

Each refund request is reviewed individually.

03

Replacement Policy

Whenever possible, we will provide a replacement instead of a refund. Eligible replacement situations include:

  • Invalid login credentials.
  • Used activation key.
  • Incorrect subscription.
  • Technical issues caused by the delivered product.
  • Product delivered with incorrect validity.
04

Non-Refundable Products

Refunds are not available for:

  • Successfully activated subscriptions.
  • Successfully redeemed activation keys.
  • Digital products already accessed.
  • Customer mistakes during ordering.
  • Incorrect email or phone number provided by the customer.
  • Products affected by customer misuse.
  • Password changes made by customers.
  • Violations of platform policies.
05

Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled before delivery. Once a digital product has been delivered, activated, or redeemed, cancellation is generally not possible.

06

Delivery Issues

If your order has not been delivered within the promised time:

  • Contact our WhatsApp Support.
  • Our team will verify the order.
  • You may receive either a replacement, or a full refund (if applicable).
    • Replacement
    • Full refund (if applicable)
07

Warranty Claims

Products with warranty are eligible for replacement according to the warranty period mentioned on the product page.

Warranty claims require
  • Order details
  • Screenshots (if applicable)
  • Description of the issue
08

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method. Typical processing time:

3–7 Business Days

Processing time may vary depending on the payment provider.

09

Contact Support

For refund requests or assistance, contact Beeway through:

  • WhatsApp Support
  • Contact Page
Please include
  • Order Number
  • Product Name
  • Payment Screenshot
  • Description of the Issue

Need help with your order?

Our support team is available to assist with refund requests, replacements, delivery issues, and warranty claims.
