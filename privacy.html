01

Introduction

At Beeway, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you interact with our website or purchase our digital products and services.

02

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Mobile Number
  • WhatsApp Number
  • Payment Information (confirmation details only)
  • Device Information
  • Browser Information
  • IP Address
  • Order History
  • Customer Support Messages
03

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process your orders
  • Deliver digital products
  • Verify payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Respond to inquiries
  • Improve our website
  • Send important service updates
  • Prevent fraud and abuse

We do not sell your personal information.

04

Payment Information

Payments are processed through secure payment methods. Beeway does not store your debit card, credit card, or banking credentials. Only transaction confirmation details required for order verification may be retained.

05

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to:

  • Improve user experience
  • Remember preferences
  • Analyze website traffic
  • Enhance website performance

Users may disable cookies through their browser settings.

06

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect customer information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

07

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade customer information. Information may only be shared when:

  • Required by law
  • Necessary for payment verification
  • Required to deliver a product
  • Needed to prevent fraud
08

Third-Party Services

Our website may use trusted third-party services including:

  • Payment Providers
  • WhatsApp
  • Google Analytics
  • Cloud Hosting Services

These providers operate under their own privacy policies.

09

Customer Rights

Customers have the right to:

  • Access personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of eligible data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Contact us regarding privacy concerns
10

Data Retention

Customer information is retained only as long as necessary for:

  • Order fulfillment
  • Customer support
  • Legal obligations
  • Fraud prevention

After that period, eligible information may be securely deleted.

11

Children's Privacy

Beeway does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If such information is identified, it will be removed promptly.

12

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes become effective immediately after publication on this page. Customers are encouraged to review this page periodically.

13

Contact Information

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us through:

  • WhatsApp Support
  • Email Support
  • Contact Page

Questions about your privacy?

If you have any questions about how your personal information is collected or used, our support team is happy to assist you.
